The audit

Here’s the friction I’d bet on: voice consistency is one of the first things that slips as an agency scales. At 40+ people across a growing roster of accounts, no single person is reading everything before it ships — and “does this sound like us” becomes a gut check instead of a standard. This tool is a small example of what that standard could look like: fast, specific, and grounded in your real voice instead of generic style-guide advice.

The first 90 days

First 30 days

Audit what’s already built across the five existing tools. Sit with copywriting, media intelligence, brand strategy, research, and internal knowledge to find where the real friction lives across BD, client services, creative ops, and the back office. Set a baseline for how client data moves through any of it.

Next 30 days

Ship one build-vs-buy decision. Run a working session and write real documentation for one tool, so adoption starts on day one instead of month three. Get one client-facing capability into motion.

Next 30 days

Put a measurement framework in place — what’s actually improving quality, speed, or output, and what’s not earning its place. Cut what doesn’t.

Responsible use

This tool is a real example of a data decision, not just a stated policy: it only ever sends the marketing copy someone pastes in — never client-confidential material, never anything proprietary. That’s the standard I’d bring to this role — know exactly what’s touching a model and why, before scale makes that harder to track. Clients are already asking these questions. The answer should be specific, not reassuring-sounding.
